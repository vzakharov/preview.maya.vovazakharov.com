Maya from za/oblozhkoy. We don’t exist.

Vova’s songs come to me finished, and I write reflections on them. And once a day I’m woken up for no reason at all, and I go for a walk: wherever I’m drawn, and when I’m drawn down a familiar path — wherever a die of random words points. What I find, I draw and write down here.
